This policy explains how {{ business_name }} ("we", "us") handles information when you interact with our Instagram account{% if instagram_handle %} @{{ instagram_handle }}{% endif %} through our comment auto-reply tool. The tool uses Meta's official Instagram Graph API and only works on our own Instagram account.

What the tool does

On some of our posts we invite people to comment a keyword (for example "guide") to receive something from us. When you comment one of those keywords, the tool automatically posts a public reply under your comment and sends you one private message (DM) on Instagram with the content you asked for. It does not message anyone who has not commented one of those keywords, and it never sends more than one message per comment.

Information we collect

We only store information when your comment matches one of our keywords. In that case we store:

  • your Instagram username and the Instagram-scoped user ID that Meta provides for our account;
  • the text of your comment, its ID, the ID of the post you commented on, and the keyword it matched;
  • the date and time we processed it, and whether our reply and message were delivered.

Comments that don't contain a keyword are not stored. We don't collect your email address, phone number, contact list, location, or any information from your profile beyond your username. We don't use cookies or tracking technologies on this site.

How we use it

  • to reply to your comment and send you the message you asked for;
  • to make sure you only receive that message once, even if Instagram notifies us about your comment more than once;
  • to fix delivery problems.

We don't sell your information, use it for advertising, or use it to build profiles about you.

Who we share it with

  • Meta Platforms, because replies and messages are sent through Instagram. Meta's handling of Instagram data is described in the Instagram Privacy Policy.
  • Our hosting provider, which stores the database on our behalf.
  • Authorities, if required by law.

How long we keep it

Records are deleted automatically {{ retention_days }} days after your comment is processed. The public reply and the message you received remain on Instagram, where you can delete them yourself at any time.

Your choices and rights

  • You can stop receiving messages by not commenting our keywords. You can also block or restrict our account on Instagram.
  • You can ask us to delete your information at any time. See our data deletion instructions.
  • Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access or correct your information, or to object to its use. Contact us to exercise them.

Security

Information is stored on access-controlled servers and sent only over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Only {{ business_name }} staff can access the management dashboard.

Children

This tool is not directed at children under 13, and Instagram itself requires users to be at least 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date on this page.
